What Rempart does

Porn is not always something you look for: it also shows up in feeds and image searches. Rempart handles both.

About 490,000 sites blocked

The OISD list, chosen for its very low error rate. Sites that label themselves as adults-only are detected and blocked automatically.

Safe searches

SafeSearch enforced on Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, Yandex and Brave, YouTube Restricted Mode, and blurred image search results.

Social networks filtered, not blocked

On Reddit, 18+ communities and NSFW posts disappear; on X, media marked as adult content. The rest of the site works as usual.

A deliberately slow unlock

No off switch. To get through anyway: a 30-minute wait, 3 written questions, a text to retype by hand and 5 captchas. Each time, the next wait doubles. The urge often passes before the end.

When in doubt, the site stays accessible

Blocking an innocent site is worse than missing one. Rempart blocks pornography and nothing else: dating sites, games, mainstream manga or torrents are not touched.

  1. A wrongly blocked site can be reported in one click from the block page: the fix helps everyone.
  2. Meanwhile, you can allow it on your device: it takes effect after 24 hours, without touching your counter.

No data collected

No account, no analytics, no ads, no Rempart server. Everything happens on your device and the code is public, so anyone can check. Privacy policy

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes, fully and for good: no subscription, no paid tier. Rempart is free software (GPL-3.0).

How do I uninstall the Windows app?

Open the Rempart app and request the uninstall code: it appears after 3 hours and stays valid for 15 minutes. The request can be cancelled. The delay lets the urge pass without locking you in.

What about Mac, Android or iPhone?

On Mac and Linux, the extension works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. An Android app and an iPhone guide are planned.

A site is blocked but has nothing pornographic.

Click “Nothing pornographic on this site?” on the block page, or report it here.
